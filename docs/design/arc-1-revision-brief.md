2. **Araknos = the Tauros bull-demon** (13ft, horns, ember eyes, greataxe, steel-turning hide) — never a spider. Confirmed. Decide rename Araknos→Tauros or keep name + bull design.
3. **Kyarias = a female necromancer at the castle gate (Arc 2)**, not an undead-army overlord. The invented beat-10 "Kyarias final boss" + `bosses/KazarthEndGame` misappropriates her.
4. **Kazarth = a friendly companion (Arc 2)**, dual-katana; the `bosses/Kazarth` sprite used for Daverak in beat 6 is a mismatch.
5. **Daverak** never falls in Book One (rules Castle Horror, undefeated) → cannot be an Arc-1 boss. His beat-6 lore role is fine (vision); asset should not be `bosses/Kazarth`.
6. **Beat 10 "Undead Army" has NO prose basis.** The prose's natural Arc-1 climax = **Darius arrives ALONE at the black-castle gate** — optionally the gate gauntlet (skeleton horde → Kyarias → Arthoros the Dragoon Knight → Balgathor halts the fight and admits him). ## Divergences that are (or may be) deliberate product-owner design — CONFIRM, don't assume
